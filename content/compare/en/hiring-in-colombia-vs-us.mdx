## The honest version of the arithmetic

The salary gap is real, and it is smaller than the pitch decks say once you count everything. A mid-level customer support lead in a US secondary market runs roughly USD 5,500 to 8,000 a month in base salary. The same seniority in Barranquilla, Medellín or Bogotá runs roughly USD 2,400 to 4,000. Add employer contributions on both sides and a one-time agency fee on the US side, and the first-year difference is usually between 45 and 60 percent.

## What you actually give up

| | Hiring in the US | Hiring in Colombia through Dexee |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Time-zone overlap | Full | Full. Colombia is UTC-5 with no daylight saving |
| Written and spoken English | Assumed | Verified against CEFR, with a human reviewer on the spoken level |
| Legal employment | Your entity, your payroll | Dexee's entity, or a contractor agreement |
| Physical presence | Possible | No |
| US licences and clearances | Possible | No |
| Time to first shortlist | Two to six weeks | Ten business days |
| Replacement if it fails | Agency terms, if any | 90 days, published in full |
| Cost of a bad hire | Salary plus the agency fee again | Replacement at no fee |

The column that matters is the last two rows. Nearshore hiring fails in the same place domestic hiring fails: the wrong person, badly onboarded. What changes is what it costs you to be wrong.

## When a US hire is still the right call

Do not hire in Colombia for a role that needs someone in a room, that carries a US professional licence, that requires a security clearance, or that holds the relationship with a regulated US client who expects a domestic counterparty. Those are not coordination problems, they are hard constraints.

There is also a soft floor. Under roughly USD 3,000 a month in US salary, the gap does not repay the effort of setting up a cross-border relationship, and you are better off hiring locally or part-time.

## What makes nearshore work

One time zone, not twelve. A verified English level rather than a claimed one. A named contact who can be in the same room as the person when something goes wrong. And a written replacement guarantee, so the downside of being wrong is bounded.
