## They solve a different problem

A global EOR platform is infrastructure. You bring a person you have already chosen, and the platform makes employing them in their country legal, paid and compliant. It is very good at that, in a hundred countries, without a sales call.

Dexee starts one step earlier. You bring a role, not a person. We find candidates, verify who they are and what their English actually is, present a shortlist, and then employ whoever you pick, in one country.

## The comparison, without the spin

| | Global EOR platform | Dexee |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Countries | 100 or more | Colombia |
| Finds the candidate | No | Yes |
| Verifies identity and English | No | Yes, with a human reviewer on spoken English |
| Monthly fee per person | USD 399 to 699 published | USD 599 published |
| Self-serve signup and API | Yes | No |
| Someone in the country who knows your person | Rarely | Yes, in Barranquilla |
| Replacement if the hire fails | Not their job | 90 days, published in full |
| Time to first shortlist | Not applicable | Ten business days |

On the monthly fee alone the two are close, and any platform that discounts hard on volume will beat us on that line. The fee is not where the difference is.

## When the platform is the better choice

If you already know who you want to hire, a platform is the cheaper and faster answer, and we will say so on the call. The same goes for a company hiring in eight countries this quarter: our single-country focus is a limitation there, not a feature.

And if your team wants to do it themselves at two in the morning without talking to anyone, a self-serve product is a real advantage. We do not have one.

## When we are the better choice

When the hard part is finding the person, not employing them. When a wrong hire costs you a quarter and you want the replacement risk on someone else's balance sheet. When the role depends on English that has to be real and not self-reported, which is where [our verification](/how-we-verify) is the actual product.

Many teams end up using both: a platform for the countries where they already have people, and Dexee for the Colombian roles they still need filled. That is a sensible outcome and we will not argue you out of it.
